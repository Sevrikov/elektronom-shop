## 5. БАЗА ДАННЫХ (Prisma + PostgreSQL 16)

### 5.1 Схема (prisma/schema.prisma)
Реализовать полную схему согласно §6 MASTER_CONTEXT v1.2:

**Модели:**
- `User` (id cuid2, email, name, phone, avatar, passwordHash, role: CUSTOMER/MANAGER/ADMIN)
- `Account` (OAuth: Google)
- `Session`
- `Address` (userId, label, firstName, lastName, phone, city, street, building, apartment, isDefault)
- `Category` (slug, parentId, sortOrder, isActive) + `CategoryTranslation` (locale, name, description, metaTitle, metaDesc)
- `Product` (slug, sku, categoryId, brandId, price, comparePrice, costPrice, stock, isActive, isFeatured, attributes JSONB, sortOrder) + `ProductTranslation` + `ProductImage`
- `Brand` (slug, name, logo, isActive)
- `Review` (productId, userId, rating 1-5, comment, isVisible)
- `WishlistItem` (userId, productId)
- `CartItem` (userId, productId, quantity) — ОБЯЗАТЕЛЬНО §14
- `Order` (number ORD-YYYY-NNNNN, userId?, addressId?, status, paymentStatus, paymentMethod, subtotal, discount, shipping, total, customerData JSON, notes)
- `OrderItem` (orderId, productId?, snapshot JSON, quantity, price)

**Индексы:**
- GIN индекс на `products.attributes` (jsonb_path_ops) — вручную после миграции
- Все @@index согласно схеме §6
